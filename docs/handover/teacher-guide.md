# Uttam Niketan — Teacher quick-start

## Sign in

1. Open **https://uttamniketan.vercel.app/login** on your phone. Tip: in the browser menu choose
   **Add to Home screen** so it opens like an app.
2. Enter the email and password the school office gave you.

## Take attendance (every school day)

1. Tap **Attendance**. Your class shows **Not taken**.
2. Tap **Take attendance**.
3. For each student tap **P** (present), **A** (absent, the row turns red), **L** (late) or
   **E** (excused).
   - Shortcut: mark the absent and late students first, then tap **Mark unmarked as present**.
4. Check the counts at the bottom, then tap **Submit** and confirm.

- Interrupted? Tap **Save draft** and finish later. Drafts are not reported to anyone.
- **Submit** stays grey until every student is marked.
- If the connection is poor, wait for "Draft saved" or the submitted screen before closing the page.
- Attendance cannot be taken for a future date.

## After submitting

- The day becomes read-only for you. If you made a mistake, tell the school office; only an admin can
  correct a submitted day, and every correction is recorded.
- If the school has switched on WhatsApp alerts, guardians of absent students who agreed to messages are
  notified automatically.

## Reports and students

- **Reports**: daily totals and each student's monthly attendance for your class.
- **Students**: your class list. Opening a student shows their last 60 days of attendance.

## Problems

- Forgot your password or "Email or password is incorrect": ask the school office to reset it.
- Your class is missing: ask the office to check your section assignment.
